Our Home: Planet Earth e

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only astronomical object known to harbor life. This is made possible by its unique positioning in the Sun's habitable zone, a rich atmosphere, and vast liquid oceans that cover about 71% of its surface. Often described as the "Blue Marble," Earth provides a dynamic, interconnected ecosystem where complex geology, climate, and biology support millions of species, making it a rare and precious oasis of life in the vast expanse of space.
